Hall of Fame

Honoring the legends of Buckeye Rugby

The Ohio State Rugby Hall of Fame recognizes players, coaches, and supporters who have made extraordinary contributions to the program. Inductees are honored annually at the Hall of Fame Banquet during Hall of Fame Weekend.
