No Experience Required

Play Rugby at Ohio State

Join one of the premier D1A college rugby programs in the nation. We welcome athletes of all backgrounds — many of our best players picked up rugby for the first time in college.

I'm Interested

Why Ohio State Rugby?

D1A Competition

Compete at the highest level of men's college rugby in the country against programs like Lindenwood, Arkansas State, and Mary Washington.

World-Class Facilities

Train on two dedicated, lighted rugby fields on campus with scoreboards. Medical support from Ohio State Medical Center travels with the team.

Brotherhood for Life

Join a community that spans nearly 60 years. Our alumni network includes Eagles, All-Americans, and lifelong friends across every profession.

Experienced Coaching

Learn from Head Coach Tom Rooney (Big Ten Coach of the Year) and a staff dedicated to developing players on and off the pitch.

Travel

Compete across the country — from Big Ten rivals to national championship tournaments. Road trips are part of the rugby experience.

All Skill Levels

Whether you played in high school or have never touched a rugby ball, there's a spot for you. We teach the game from the ground up.

What to Expect

Fall Season (August – November)

15s rugby. Conference matches in the Big Ten (transitioning to Midwest D1A in 2026), cross-conference games, and the CRAA Challenger Cup.

Spring Season (February – May)

15s conference play, 7s tournaments, and the CRAA Championship weekend. Spring is when the team peaks for postseason competition.

Practice

Practices are held multiple times per week at Lincoln Tower Fields. All sessions are coached and structured for player development at every level.

Commitment

Rugby is a club sport, so it's designed to coexist with your academics. We're serious about competing at the highest level, but we understand you're students first.
