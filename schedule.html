Postseason — CRAA Challenger Cup

Ohio State is competing in the 2025-26 CRAA Challenger Cup. The Buckeyes defeated Air Force Academy in the quarterfinals, building a 23-0 lead before AFA rallied.

Championship weekend is scheduled for May 2026 in Indianapolis.

Conference Change — Fall 2026

Beginning Fall 2026, Ohio State will compete in the Midwest D1A Conference alongside Lindenwood, Arkansas State, Davenport, and McKendree. This move aligns the program fully with CRAA for a clearer path to the national championship.
