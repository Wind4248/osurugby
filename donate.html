Why Your Support Matters

As a club sport, Ohio State Rugby receives no funding from the Athletics Department. Everything — equipment, travel, league and referee fees, field maintenance, medical support — is funded by alumni society donations and player dues.

Your contribution directly impacts the student-athletes who carry on a tradition that began in 1966.

Gavin Gill Memorial Scholarship Fund

Gavin Gill Memorial Scholarship

In memory of Gavin Gill (RRHS '17, OSU '21), who played rugby all four years at Ohio State. Gavin passed away on June 27, 2021, from complications of Type 1 diabetes.

The Gavin Gill Foundation provides scholarships to students with Type 1 diabetes at Rocky River High School and beyond. Your donations to the Alumni Society's scholarship fund honor Gavin's legacy every year at the Gavin Gill Rugby Festival.
